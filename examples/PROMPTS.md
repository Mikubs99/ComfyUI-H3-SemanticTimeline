# Example natural-language prompts

No timeline syntax is required. Feed any prompt below to Preview or the main node.

## Linear action
A woman in a black sweater sits on a sofa speaking to camera. She stands up,
walks toward the window while continuing to speak, looks outside, then turns back
to camera.

## Prompt Enhancer style
A cinematic medium shot of a woman wearing a black sweater sitting on a sofa in a
warmly lit living room. She speaks naturally to the camera before standing up and
slowly walking toward the window while continuing her conversation. She reaches
the window, looks outside for a moment, then turns back toward the camera. Soft
ambient room tone, realistic movement, natural skin texture and warm afternoon light.

## Dialogue
A woman sits on a sofa and says "Welcome home!" She stands up and walks to the window.
At the window she looks outside and says “The rain has stopped.” Then she turns
back toward the camera. A continuous shot, quiet room ambience, natural voice.

## Camera progression
A woman stands beside the sofa. The camera holds a medium shot as she starts walking
to the window, then slowly tracks beside her. She stops at the window and looks
outside. The camera gently pans to frame the garden beyond the glass.

## Static atmosphere
A woman in a black sweater is seated in a warmly lit living room, relaxed posture,
a still medium shot, quiet room tone, realistic cinematic texture.

## Long 30–60 second progression
In one continuous shot, a woman in a black sweater sits on a sofa and speaks calmly
to the camera. After a thoughtful pause she stands up, slowly crosses the living
room while continuing her conversation, reaches the window and watches the garden
for a while. She gently places her hand on the windowsill, then turns back toward
the camera and smiles. Warm afternoon lighting and soft room ambience remain steady.
